    Three things to know before you start

    Write it rough

    Type your idea the way you'd say it. MetaPrompt turns it into a structured prompt — role, task, method and constraints — for any AI you use.

    Keep secrets out

    Your text goes straight from this browser to the provider you connect. Leave passwords and private data out of what you paste.

    Fill the gaps

    Missing details become [PLACEHOLDERS] instead of guesses. Read the prompt and fill them in before you use it.

    Choose your prompt depth

    Short

    ~600characters

    Tight and concise — the core of the request.

    • Role, task and constraints only
    • No rubric or worked example
    • Fastest to read and paste

    Medium

    600–1,500characters

    Balanced — enough structure for most tasks.

    • Role, task, method and constraints
    • Plain-language clarity rule
    • Placeholders for missing details
    • Recommended for everyday use

    Long

    1,500–3,500characters

    Detailed — for work that needs a full plan.

    • Everything in Medium
    • Staged workflow with checkpoints
    • Evaluation rubric for the answer
    • An example when it helps

    Max

    Completeup to 8,192 tokens

    Everything — for high-stakes or long-running work.

    • Everything in Long
    • All sections and examples
    • Verification protocol
    • Uses the most output tokens

    Using Anthropic. Longer prompts use more output tokens on your own key. Change the provider in Settings.
